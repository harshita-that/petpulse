# PetPulse — Between Vet Visits

> Pet health tracking that spots problems early using photo analysis.

---

## The Problem

You brush your teeth every day. You floss (sometimes). You catch cavities early because you know what to look for.

But your dog? You have no idea what's happening inside that mouth until the vet says it's a **$1,200 dental surgery**.

Pet health shouldn't be a guessing game you lose.

---

## The Solution

**Between Vet Visits** lets you photograph your pet's teeth, eyes, skin, or gait and get **instant health insights** powered by visual analysis.

The subtle warning signs surface early. The stuff you'd miss on your own becomes obvious.

- That weird bump
- That cloudy eye
- That slight limp

**Caught in weeks instead of months.**

---

## How It Works

1. **Snap** — Take a photo of your pet's teeth, eyes, skin, or gait
2. **Scan** — AI-powered visual analysis identifies subtle health changes
3. **Track** — Monitor trends over time with health scores and timelines
4. **Act** — Get actionable insights and know when to see the vet
